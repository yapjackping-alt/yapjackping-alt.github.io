Privacy Policy for EmberDoom (余烬末日 / Emberfall)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

EmberDoom is a free game made by Yap Jack Ping (the "developer"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information stored on your device

Your characters, progress, items and settings are saved only on your own device. The developer cannot see this data.

2. Information sent to our server (global leaderboard)

To run the global leaderboard, the game uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted by Google in Singapore). When you play, the game automatically:

  • creates a guest account for your device (a random user ID; no password, no email);
  • uploads your leaderboard entry: the character nickname you chose, character level, combat power, number of zombies defeated, best endless-wave result and the time of the update.

If you choose to sign in with Google (available in some versions), Google shares your Google account ID and email address with the game so your account can be kept when you change phones. The email is not shown to other players.

Your nickname and leaderboard numbers are visible to all players on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or personal details as your nickname.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone other than Google, which stores it for us as our service provider. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

5. Deleting your data

To delete your leaderboard entry and guest/Google account data, email the developer at the address below with your nickname and the guest ID shown in the game (Activities → Leaderboard). We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on your device.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

Yap Jack Ping – yapjackping@gmail.com
